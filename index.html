    Behind the film

      Accessibility

      Works with a keyboard, a screen reader or a thumb

      • Every control by keyboard

        Tab reaches each button, slider and menu option in the order you see them, and every focused part gets the same white ring.

      • Clear names for screen readers

        Buttons say what they do, like “Seek forward 10 seconds”. The seek ring is a slider that reads out “1:04 of 14:48”.

      • Subtitles in two languages

        Each film has English and Spanish tracks. Turn them on from the options menu or press C.

      • Respects reduced motion

        With reduced motion on in your system settings, the fan-out, springs and wheel animations are switched off.

      • Big enough to hit

        Option buttons are 44 px. Sliders have a hit area taller than the line you see, and nothing overlaps on touch screens.

      • More than colour

        When something is on, its icon changes shape and gets a dot, so subtitles, casting and full screen never rely on colour alone.

      Features

      One round control instead of a control bar

      • Seek around the ring

        Hover the ring to preview any moment from the Mux storyboard. Drag around it to scrub, with the time following your finger.

      • Click-wheel volume

        Tap the bottom button for a slider, or press and hold it and circle clockwise to turn the volume up, like an iPod.

      • Options that fan out

        The top button opens subtitles, settings, cast, picture in picture and full screen, each with its own panel above the control.

      • Streamed from Mux

        Each film plays as adaptive HLS from Mux, so picture quality follows your connection, and the seek previews come from its storyboards.

      • Spin to switch films

        Turn the dial at the bottom of the screen. The films swing round with it and the player picks up where the wheel stops.

      • Put the control where you like

        Choose small, medium or large, and centre, bottom or corner, in Settings. Orbit remembers your choice in this browser.
